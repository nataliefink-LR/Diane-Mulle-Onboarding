# Slack
Slack — How We Communicate
LiveRamp is a Slack-first company. This is where the work happens.

At LiveRamp, Slack is our primary communication tool — email is largely reserved for external candidates and formal communications. Default to Slack for questions, quick updates, and team collaboration. A few norms to know: keep conversations in public team channels when possible (it helps everyone stay in the loop), use threads to keep channels tidy, and set your status when you're heads-down or OOO. Ask Natalie or Meghan to add you to the right channels your first day.
